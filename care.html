How Should We Care for Animals?

Every animal deserves food, clean water, a safe shelter and proper medical attention.

🥗

Healthy Food

Provide suitable food according to the animal's needs.

💧

Clean Water

Make sure animals have access to fresh, clean water.

🏠

Safe Shelter

Provide a clean, comfortable and safe living place.

🩺

Health Care

Visit a veterinarian for checkups and necessary treatment.

Important Care Tips

  • Keep the animal's living area clean.
  • Give regular exercise where appropriate.
  • Never hurt or abandon animals.
  • Provide vaccinations and medical care as recommended.
  • Treat animals with kindness and patience.
