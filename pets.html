What Are Domestic Pets?

Domestic pets are animals that people keep as companions. They provide love, friendship and happiness.

🐶

Dogs

Dogs are loyal, friendly and playful animals.

🐱

Cats

Cats are independent, curious and affectionate.

🐰

Rabbits

Rabbits are gentle animals that need proper care.

🐦

Birds

Pet birds need clean cages, fresh food and space.

Benefits of Having Pets

  • Pets provide companionship.
  • They can reduce feelings of loneliness.
  • They teach us responsibility.
  • They encourage play and physical activity.
