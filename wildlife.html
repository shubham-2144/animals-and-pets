What Are Wild Animals?

Wild animals live in natural habitats such as forests, grasslands, deserts and oceans.

🦁

Lion

Lions are carnivores that mainly live in African grasslands.

🐘

Elephant

Elephants are large, intelligent and social mammals.

🐯

Tiger

Tigers are powerful carnivores found in parts of Asia.

🦒

Giraffe

Giraffes use their long necks to reach leaves on tall trees.

Why Are Wild Animals Important?

  • They help maintain the balance of ecosystems.
  • They are important parts of food chains.
  • They help support biodiversity.
  • They must be protected from habitat loss and hunting.
